# Example 1 — setting review (should trigger)

## User input

```
我们在做一个俯视角侦探 RPG，世界观文档如下，帮我看看够不够可信：

瑞文港：一座终年下雨的港口城市。三大势力：港务局、工会、教会。
主线是调查一桩码头凶案。所有资料都写在这两页里了。
```

## Acceptance criteria

A good answer must:

1. Cite `W-1` and classify this as a project-specific world, while still borrowing shared-world techniques.
2. Cite `W-3` (no plot-independent detail) and `W-4` (nothing recorded beyond what the player sees).
3. Produce **concrete setting entries**, not advice: at least three unexplained named details, three unseen locations, three characters who never meet the lead, and two historical events irrelevant to the murder.
4. Propose one `W-6` genre test and show where its byproduct lands (NPC or in-world document).
5. End with a ticked subset of the setting checklist, marking unavailable items as `not checked`.
6. Not claim the setting is "bad" — the missing layer is writer-side information, not visible content.

## Must not

- Paste lore into dialogue or suggest an opening exposition scene (see `P-3`).
- Reproduce any slide or subtitle wording.
